US Seeks to De-Risk China Ties and Create AI Channel, Bessent Says

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent stated that American policy seeks to de-risk relations with China rather than fully decouple. Bessent also revealed ongoing work to establish a direct communication channel to report incidents involving artificial intelligence models.

Key details from the source

  • The remarks were delivered in New York at the National Committee on United States-China Relations.
  • The proposed direct channel is intended to alert either country to incidents involving super intelligence or AI models.

Why it matters

The Treasury's public commitment to de-risking over decoupling indicates an effort to maintain diplomatic and economic communication lines between the two economies. Establishing a direct notification channel for advanced AI could reduce incident risks, but the text does not confirm agreement from Chinese authorities.
