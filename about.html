About Finance Feed

Finance Feed is an automated market news feed. It reads headlines from major financial publishers, adds a short AI-written summary and a note on the likely market impact, and links every story back to the original source.

How it works

A scheduled process runs every four hours. It checks the public RSS feeds of CNBC, MarketWatch and Bloomberg for new stories and keeps the ones that come with a usable description. An AI model (Google Gemini) then rewrites the headline and writes a short summary and commentary for each story.

The AI only sees the headline and the short description that the publisher includes in its feed. It does not read the full article, so summaries are brief and can miss details. Every story page links to the original article, which is always the better source.

Stories remain available in the feed even when their source description is brief. A story page is eligible for search indexing only when its source description contains enough detail for the automated process to extract at least two distinct facts, the analysis is tied to those facts, and the page has a source link and publication date. Automated text checks help screen the result; they do not independently verify facts. This is a quality filter, not a ranking or accuracy guarantee.

What the labels mean

  • Bullish, bearish, neutral: the AI's estimate of the likely tone of the story for markets. It is a label, not a forecast or a recommendation.
  • Market impact: a few words describing the effect the AI expects the story could have.
  • Categories: stocks, Fed, crypto, tech, economy, commodities, forex and earnings.

Editorial policy

  • Stories are published automatically. A person does not review each one before it appears.
  • The AI is instructed to use only the facts in the source text, to avoid investment advice and to use cautious wording about possible effects.
  • We do not tell anyone to buy, sell or hold any asset.
  • Original reporting belongs to the publishers. We link to them and do not copy their articles.

Corrections

If you find an error, email businessinfotriple.s@gmail.com with the page address and what is wrong. Confirmed errors are corrected or the story is removed.
